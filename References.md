# References

The following independent sources were retained from Part 1 as planning benchmarks for shelter content, foster-care communication and responsible animal-welfare information architecture.

1. Citizens for Animal Protection. [CAP website](https://www.cap4pets.org/). Accessed 21 September 2026.
2. Best Friends Animal Society. [Foster Programs Training Playbook](https://bestfriends.org/network/resources-tools/foster-programs-training-playbook). Accessed 21 September 2026.
3. Association of Shelter Veterinarians. [Guidelines for Standards of Care in Animal Shelters](https://www.sheltervet.org/guidelines-for-standards-of-care-in-animal-shelters). Accessed 21 September 2026.

These sources informed the prominence of adoption, foster care, volunteering, donations, contact pathways and responsible-care messaging. The website copy remains an academic prototype and does not claim to represent the referenced organisations.
