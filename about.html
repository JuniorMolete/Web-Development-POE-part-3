Our purpose

About the shelter

Compassionate care and practical support help animals move from crisis to a safe, permanent home.

What we do

Paws Community Animal Shelter is a non-profit animal rescue organisation focused on the welfare of domestic animals in need.

Our work includes rescue, medical care, behavioural support, foster-care coordination and adoption services. We also promote vaccinations, microchipping, sterilisation and responsible pet ownership.

Our community

Potential adopters, foster carers, volunteers, donors and local families all play an important role in helping animals move from crisis to a safe, permanent home.

Research-informed approach

This prototype uses independent animal-welfare research as a planning benchmark. Clear adoption, foster, volunteer, donation and contact pathways make support easier to find.
